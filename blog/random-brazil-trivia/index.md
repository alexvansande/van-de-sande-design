Brazil’s first war as an independent country was with also newly independent Argentina, over who would control a bordering province. Both countries lost and thus was created the independent country of Uruguay.

Brazil still has some territorial disputes with Uruguay. One of them is a river island which was until 2011 inhabited by a single, ninety year old, farmer. When a fire broke there in 2009, firefighters from both countries came to the rescue.  It’s currently uninhabited.

Chile is almost as wide as its tall. In fact it's as close to a perfect square  ratio (1.03)  as Egypt, often considered the "squarest of countries".

![](24.jpg)

Some friends questioned the calculation. There are many ways to say "wide" as there are no rectangles in a sphere. When measuring the great circle distance from westernmost point (Moto Nui) to easternmost point (Terra del Fuego), Chile is actually wider than tall!
