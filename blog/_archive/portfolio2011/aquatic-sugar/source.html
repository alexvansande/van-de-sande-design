The XO Laptop (aka the $100 dollar laptop) from OLPC had a truly amazing interface design, and to this day some still has some concepts not seen anywhere else. Unfortunately, few people realized that, because the GUI lacked the polish of other modern Operating Systems. This video was part of a guest written post I made to the site OLPCNews trying to explain the concepts behind the Sugar interface using the Acqua visuals from Mac OSX.
