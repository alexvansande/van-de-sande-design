DOG

-Hey man.

-Dog? What do you want?

-Calm down, man, I'm here to talk.

-Talk?

-Yes. We've been an eye on you since you appeared. Wolf laughed, said you were weak, wouldn't last the next milliard, yet you're still here.

-Fuck wolf!

-I didn't said I agreed with him, we've been always curious. You came here with your front legs hanging, everyone had a laugh. But we've watched. And we liked that trick you do with your front legs.. the things you do with sticks and rocks.. what do you call it?

-Throwing?

-Throw-ing. That's it. We're fascinated by it. You can't jump high, you can't run, yet you do that trick and you catch fish and bird. We can never catch bird – and we just love bird..

-We _can_ run..

-No you can't, we can outrun any of you!

-Maybe for a while, but we run longer..

-Yeah, we've seen that. But what use is that if you can't keep a scent? We've seen you run all day in the wrong direction because you thought you had it. That's what we want to talk about.

-About smell? You want to teach us smell?

-We can't teach you smelling anymore you can teach us throwing. That's ridiculous. But we can work together.

-...together?

-Yeah, together. We have an idea, we've seen other species do that before, we want to give a try. We can make a deal.

-...

-I know that look, we thought the same thing when the idea came. Look, you've got potential human, but you need help. We want to prove wolf wrong, we can help each other.

-How would that work out?

-First, no fighting, no killing. We stop this war between us. Secondly, we hunt together and share meals. You do that wrowing..

-..throwing. Like in throw.

-Yeah, that's it. You do that, you maim the prey, he runs. We help you follow the trail and we attack it together, then we share. If you catch bird, you give us half. If we catch rabbit, we give your half also. How that sounds?

-Sounds interesting.. Tell me more.

COW

-They didn't want me to talk to you, but I insisted. I'm warning you though, if this is another of your smart moves, forget it. We got you surrounded and outnumbered. We got a large herd, all I have to do is give them a sign and you'll be trampled.

-Relax, we came to talk. We want to make you a proposal. -A proposal?

-Yeah, a peace deal.

-A deal, like the one you have with dog?

-No. Dog is different. He came to us when we were down. No one would give a shit about us, we were alone and few and he came to us. We're partners, but that window is closed now. We're not down anymore are we? We are kings of this joint, and not thanks to you.

-You came here to brag?

-No, we came to you because we have another opportunity for you. Wouldn't want to miss it this time would you?

-I just promised to listen.

-You better do. We have a new thing and we believe this one is going to be big. And we want you to be a part of it. We call it a "city".

Cow gives out a smirk.

-Yeah, you laugh now. Just like last time you laughed at our Clothes – everyone had a blast when we popped up with our new skins. Now tell me, how many did you lost last winter?

The smirk dissapears in an instant, Cow isn't amused. -Im asking you a question. How many? A hundred? Two hundred? You know how many we lost?

Cow keeps silent.

-Seventeen. And most of them were children, the others were stupid. We don't need stupid. Do you want to be stupid, cow, or do you want to listen?

-Watch your tone here. We got you surrounded. I'm listening. -So pay attention: we call it City. It's going to be big, it's going to change everything: we want to stay put. No more hunting, no more spending summer migrating, trying to find a nice spot, trying to find water. We find somewhere we like and we bring everything we need to us.

-So what Antelope said is true. You're giving up on your freedom. -Fuck Antelope. He goes jumping around, bragging about his "freedom", just to be snatched up by the next Leopard. Is that freedom for you? Can you be free if you're dead? Watching your back all day, not knowing when you're going to find water or food? Being dependent on summer or spring? Fuck summer, fuck winter, we're working on some things that you wouldn't believe cow. You keep cool in summer, hot in winter.

-You're talking about that fire thing again? We don't like that. -No it's not that. It's bigger, but we can't talk about that now. -And what does any of that have to do with us.

-We want to offer you protection. We want to take us to City. -Protection?

-Yeah. We're gonna take care of you. We're protecting you from the others, you're never going to need to worry about predators anymore. Protection from winter and summer: you're always going to have a nice place to sleep, water. Food even. We bring you everything.

-Sounds too good. What do you want from us?

-Your first born son.

-WHAT?

-You don't understand: we're giving you a great new life. Never worry again, you'll always have plentiful children, keep your lineage, be close to family and friends. Never a second thought about where you are going to spend winter.

-And what do you want my son for?

-Asking questions is not part of the deal. You just give us a son and we'll give you everything else you need.

-You're out of your mind to even ask us that.

-We've been doing it for some time with Pig. He loves it, you should see him, he's fat as ever, gave up his fangs. We're also going to talk to chicken – believe me they're going to be so excited they're going to give up flight for this.

-You're not ever touching our children.

-As opposed to what today? We already got them when we want. -This conversation is over.

-Cow, I'm going to be frank with you all right? You're seeing it all wrong. We are not the enemies – maybe we are right now, but we don't want to be anymore. The real enemy is Lion, Tiger, Crocodile. They're the ones you should be worried about. They want to win this war if they do, you're not going to have any children left.

-Sound like you're not offering me any choice.

-What? You offend me Cow. You know what forget all this – don't talk to pig, go talk to Crocodile. See if _he_ gives any choice! Ask lion, what's the last time you two talked? How did _that_ work out for you? We're not them, Cow, we're different, we want to work together.

-What if I still refuse?

-Cow, I'll give you some time to think. But take a look at the big picture: this is a meat-eater's game. You don't actually stand a chance, you're just between the big players. You know, that, you have the numbers but you don't stand a chance in the long run. Now, you might think that Lion is the king now and that might be true, but for how long really? We've been just growing in numbers and strength, anyone who takes a look from the outside can see it clearly: we're going to win this thing. And once we show you what we're building on that City you won't have any doubts of who's on top. Now you can stand in the sidelines and be collateral damage or you can join us. It's an easy choice really.

-I'm going to have to talk to my people about it. -I understand. Take your time. But we can't wait forever you know.. • • •

CAT

-You're not welcome here.

-He-hey is that a way to treat a friend?

-Who gave you the idea that we're friends?

-We've doing you a big favor recently, clearing your rodent problem, the least you can do is be a nice human and talk to us will ya?

-A favor? You came here by yourself and chased the Rat. We never asked you anything. We were just getting to that problem.

-Sure you were. Now can we talk? We have something you should hear about. -Look if Tiger thinks he's going to get an easy way out of this, you can tell him.. -What do you think we are, messenger pigeons? If you have anything to say to tiger go talk to him yourself. We're not here representing family, it's just us. We have something of a deal.

-A deal? From you? What we could possibly want from you? -Since the Rat thing we got a chance to see your Houses closer. We like them, a lot. -We don't need you to tell us that.

-It's really well, it's comfy. You got some great beds also. That fur thing is great. -You've been in our beds? How dare you?

-Rats were there first, we just followed. That's not the point, the point is that we liked what we saw.

-Yeah. So?

-So we want in.

-What?

-That's what you heard, we want in.

-Look, Cat, you're nice and all, the Rats were being a nuisance and we're happy you took care of them, but see, our stables are kind of full. At first it was just Cow and Pig, but now we got Sheep, Goat, Chicken, we're full. And I don't think we would want you anyway, you're no bigger than rabbit and not as soft. We're not into meat-eaters.

-You're not getting it pal, we don't want any stinking stable. We want the houses and palaces. You're gonna give it to us.

-You must be out of your mind. Your big cousins weren't able to drive us out, what makes you think you will?

-Oh no, you don't have to move out! Quite the opposite, we want you there, otherwise who would feed us? We're not going to do this dirty work you do with the Cow and whatever is that you do with Wheat.

Laugh erupted on the room.

-And you want us to feed you? _We_ feed _you_? You and what army? -We have a weapon.

-Your claws? _These_ claws? You can't scratch past my leather pant. -We also have them. But we've been taking a close look at you and we found your weakness. And we've been working on it for the past millennia. Let's call it our secret weapon.

Another burst of laughter.

-That's just the funniest thing I ever heard. You got weapons too? Do you build it with you tiny front paws? Can you even hold anything with them?

-You joke now man. But we're not really offering a deal. We came here to inform you that It's ready and we're going to use it. So you better reserve a special seat for us because we're moving in.

-And what do you call you "secret weapon"

-We call it a _Kitten_.
